# Big Fast Value Framework

## Core Concept

Give massive value BEFORE asking for anything. The "reward" must be tangible, specific, and immediately useful.

---

## The Lead Magnet Strategy

**Do NOT sell the software in this post.**

**Instead reward attention with:**
- Custom Lead-Leak Report for their specific listings
- Data-backed analysis of their market
- Proof of what's possible (case study)

---

## The "How I" vs "How To" Framework

### ❌ Wrong:
"How to automate your real estate leads"

### ✅ Right:
"How I tracked lead decay for 50 listings in {City} and what I found"

---

## Authority Positioning

Use these authority-builders:

1. **Specific numbers**: "50 listings", "30 days", "$20k commission"
2. **First-person results**: "I tracked", "I found", "I measured"
3. **Third-party proof**: "391% more likely to convert" (cite the study)
4. **Scarcity with honesty**: "I can only pull 3 reports this week because the tracking is manual"

---

---

## Give:Ask Ratio (Strict Rules)

**Instagram:**
- 4 value-driven carousels for every 1 "Ask" post
- Never ask for follows, likes, or shares
- Single CTA: "DM 'LEAK' to see your report"

**Threads:**
- 10 pieces of pure "math breakdown" for every 1 offer post
- Educational content only until the offer post
- Same single CTA: "Drop 'LEAK'"

---

## The 391% Stat Usage

**Source context:**
"According to research by Zillow, leads contacted within 60 seconds are 391% more likely to convert."

**How to use it:**
- Always cite Zillow/research as the source
- Pair with the 60-second response time
- Contrast with "average agent response time of 3+ hours"

---

## Hormozi Guardrails Applied

1. **Big value FIRST** - Give the framework, not just the offer
2. **Fast to consume** - Each slide/post must stand alone
3. **Specific over generic** - "50 listings in Austin" not "some agents"
4. **Reality-based scarcity** - "3 reports this week because tracking is manual"
5. **Single action** - Only one CTA per post
