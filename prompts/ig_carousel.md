# Instagram Carousel Content Prompt

## Task
Generate a 5-7 slide carousel for Instagram targeting data-wary real estate agents.

---

## Input Parameters
- **City:** {CITY}
- **Framework:** {FRAMEWORK} (magic_headline | salty_pretzel | big_fast_value)
- **Target ICP:** data_wary_shark

---

## Slide Structure

### Slide 1: The Hook (Most Important)
- **Framework: magic_headline**
  - M: "Manual Lead-Flow Audit"
  - A: "For Top 1% {CITY} Agents"
  - G: "Capture the $20k Missed Commission"
  - I: "From the Last 30 Days"
- **Design:** Bold headline, high-contrast, circle the problem

### Slide 2: The Problem
- The nightmare scenario
- "3 AM inquiry → 9 AM 'found another agent'"
- Use CRM mockup visual concept

### Slide 3: The Rival
- "Your competitor is closing the deals you're missing"
- "They respond in under 60 seconds"
- Contrast: You vs. Them

### Slide 4: The Math
- 1 missed lead × $20k ÷ 30 days = $667/day
- 391% more likely to convert when contacted in <60 seconds
- Visual: Numbers in bold financial report style

### Slide 5: The Narrow Solution
- "A Custom Lead-Leak Report"
- "I can show you exactly where YOUR leads are going cold"
- Authority: "How I tracked 50 listings"

### Slide 6: The BIG Reveal (Optional)
- "Once you see the leak, here's how to fix it 24/7"
- Transition to ReplyzeAI (but don't sell yet)

### Slide 7: The Ask (Only One CTA)
- "DM 'LEAK' to see your report"
- Ethical scarcity: "Only pulling 3 this week"
- High-contrast CTA design

---

## Design Specifications

- **Resolution:** 1080x1350 (IG Portrait)
- **Colors:** Dark background (#1a1a1a), Neon green (#00ff88), Red accent (#ff3366)
- **Fonts:** Bold sans-serif (mimic financial reports)
- **Visual elements:**
  - Circle problem areas in bright red
  - Highlight opportunities in neon green
  - Use "leak" metaphor visually
