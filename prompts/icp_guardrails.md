# ICP: Data-Wary Real Estate Sharks

## Who They Are

**Title:** Top 1% Real Estate Agents  
**Self-Image:** "Data-driven professionals" / "Real estate sharks"  
**Core Fear:** Missing opportunities because of information gaps  
**Core Desire:** Systems that give them an unfair advantage  

---

## What They Care About

### ✅ DO Use:
- **Data and statistics** - Cite sources, be specific ("391%", "60 seconds")
- **ROI language** - "$20k commission", "$667/day", "conversion rate"
- **Efficiency metrics** - Response time, lead decay, close rates
- **Competitor positioning** - "Your rival is closing deals you're missing"
- **Proof over promises** - Case studies, real numbers, actual results
- **Scarcity with logic** - "I can only do 3 reports because the tracking is manual"

### ❌ DO NOT Use:
- **Vague claims** - "More leads!" without numbers
- **Emotional appeals** - Focus on data, not feelings
- **Generic advice** - Must be specific to their market
- **Multiple CTAs** - One action only
- **"Get rich quick" framing** - They're sophisticated investors

---

## How They Consume Content

**Instagram:**
- Carousels with bold headlines
- Data visualizations
- CRM mockups (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Zillow)
- High-contrast design (dark backgrounds, neon accents)

**Threads:**
- Text-first, math-focused
- Thread format with bullet points
- "Here's the math" approach
- Short paragraphs, high information density

---

## The Lead Magnet They Want

**Name:** "Custom Lead-Leak Report"  
**Format:** Data analysis of their specific listings  
**Value:** Shows them exactly where leads are going cold  
**Scarcity:** "Only 3 reports this week"  

---

## Response Triggers

They respond when content:
1. Shows a specific number they're missing
2. Contrasts them with a rival who is winning
3. Offers proof through a free audit/report
4. Respects their intelligence with data

---

## Message Framing

### Problem Framing:
"You think your problem is X"  
"While you're doing Y, your rival is doing Z"  
"The math says you're losing $X per day"  

### Solution Framing:
"Here's exactly what's happening"  
"This is how I tracked it"  
"If you want to see YOUR data, here's how to get it"  

### CTA Framing:
"Drop 'LEAK'" - Simple, data-focused ask  
"DM 'REPORT'" - Transactional but valuable  
"Comment your address" - Shows they're serious  

---

## What Makes Them Trust

1. **Specificity** - "50 listings in Austin" not "real estate agents"
2. **Transparency** - "The tracking is manual, so I can only do 3"
3. **Data sourcing** - Cite Zillow, real studies
4. **Results language** - "I found", "I measured", "I tracked"
5. **Respect for their time** - Single CTA, no fluff

---
