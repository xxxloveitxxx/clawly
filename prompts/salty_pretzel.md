# The Salty Pretzel Framework

## Core Concept

Solve the NARROW problem (identifying the leak) to reveal the BIGGER problem (responding fast enough to win).

---

## Visual Design

- **High-contrast colors**: Bright UI elements on dark backgrounds
- **CRM mockups**: Screenshots of Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Zillow lead alerts
- **Circle leaks**: Use bright red or neon green to highlight missed opportunities
- **Resolution**: 1080x1350 (IG Portrait), 1080x1080 (Threads)
- **Typography**: Bold, sans-serif fonts mimicking financial reports

---

## Content Structure

### **The Pretzel (Curiosity Driver)**

```
You think your problem is: "Not enough leads"

Your ACTUAL problem is: "You can't respond fast enough to win the leads you already have"

The narrow solve: "A Custom Lead-Leak Report"

The big reveal: "ReplyzeAI responds 24/7 in under 60 seconds"
```

### **Timeline Math (The What-Who-When)**

**What happens to a Zillow lead:**
- 3:00 AM - Inquiry comes in
- 3:05 AM - You're asleep, lead is still "hot"
- 3:10 AM - 2 other agents have already responded
- 9:00 AM - You check email, "already found an agent"
- **Result**: You lost a $20k commission to someone who just responded faster

**Who benefits:**
- Your rival agent who has auto-responders set up
- They didn't work harder - they just reacted faster
- Now they're collecting the commission you earned

### **The Salty Pretzel Bridge**

```
Narrow Problem: "I don't know where my leads are leaking"
        ↓
Narrow Solution: "Get a Custom Lead-Leak Report"  
        ↓
BIG Problem: "Even if I knew, I can't be online 24/7 to respond"
        ↓
BIG Solution: "ReplyzeAI handles it automatically"
```

## Hormozi Guardrails Applied

1. **Proof over promises** - Use "391% more likely to convert" stat
2. **The Rival POV** - "They" are winning, not you
3. **Narrow to BIG** - Don't sell the software in the first post
4. **Ethical scarcity** - "Only 3 reports this week because tracking is manual"
5. **Single CTA** - Never ask for likes, follows, or multiple actions
