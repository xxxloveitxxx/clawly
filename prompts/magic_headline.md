# M.A.G.I.C. Headline Framework

## The Hook Formula

Every piece of content must follow this structure:

### **M - Magnet**
"Custom Lead-Flow Audit"

### **A - Avatar**  
"For Top 1% {City} Agents"

### **G - Goal**
"Capture the $20k Missed Commission"

### **I - Interval**
"From the Last 30 Days"

### **C - Credibility**
Use real data points: "391% higher conversion", "60-second response", "5-minute decay"

---

## Instagram Carousel Structure

**Slide 1: The Hook**
- Bold headline using M.A.G.I.C. formula
- High-contrast design (dark bg, neon accents)
- Circle problem areas in bright red/neon green

**Slide 2: The Problem**
- Nightmarish scenario: "A Zillow lead that went cold in 10 minutes"
- Use actual CRM mockup visuals
- Show the math: "1 missed lead × $20k commission = ???"

**Slide 3: The Rival**
- "Your competitor is closing the deals you're missing"
- "They respond in under 60 seconds"
- "Meanwhile, you're checking voicemails at 9 AM"

**Slide 4: The Solution Preview**
- "What if you knew exactly where every lead leaked?"
- "Custom Lead-Leak Report for {City} listings"
- Authority positioning: "How I tracked lead decay for 50 listings"

**Slide 5: The Ask**
- Single CTA: "DM 'LEAK' to see your report"
- Ethical scarcity: "Only pulling 3 reports this week"

---

## Threads Post Structure

**Hook Line:**
"Your competitor is closing deals you're missing. Here's why..."

**Body:**
- Problem/Solution bridge
- The 3-5-9 timeline (3 AM inquiry → 5 AM missed → 9 AM "found another agent")
- Data point: "391% more likely to convert when contacted in under 60 seconds"

**CTA:**
"Drop 'LEAK' and I'll analyze your top listing for free."

---

## Guardrails from Hormozi Books

1. **Over-give before asking** - 4 value posts for every 1 ask
2. **Use real data, not opinions** - Cite sources, use actual percentages
3. **Ethical scarcity** - "I can only do 3 of these" is real limitation
4. **The Rival mindset** - Write from competitor's perspective
5. **Speed matters** - Emphasize the 60-second window constantly
