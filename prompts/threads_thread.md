## Thread Structure

### Opening Hook (1-2 sentences)
- Must stop the scroll immediately
- Data-driven or contrarian
- Examples:
  - "You're not short on leads. You're short on speed."
  - "Your competitor is closing deals you're missing. Here's the math that proves it."
  - "I tracked 50 listings in {CITY} for 30 days. What I found will change how you think about lead response."

### Problem Setup (2-3 posts)
- The nightmare timeline
- "3 AM inquiry → 9 AM 'found another agent'"
- Contrast ideal vs. reality

### The Math (3-4 posts)
- 5 minutes: average lead goes cold
- 3-4 hours: average agent response time
- 391%: conversion boost with <60 second response
- The daily/weekly/monthly cost of missed leads

### The Rival (1-2 posts)
- "They're not working harder. They're just faster."
- "The agent winning in your market is winning on speed, not skill."

### The Solution Bridge (2-3 posts)
- Narrow problem: "You don't know where your leads are leaking"
- BIG problem: "Even if you knew, you can't be online 24/7"
- The narrow solution: "A Custom Lead-Leak Report"

### The Offer (1 post)
- "I can analyze your top listing for free"
- "Only doing 3 reports this week because tracking is manual"
- "If you're moving serious volume in {CITY}, you're probably leaking leads"

### CTA (1 post)
- Single action: "Drop 'LEAK'"
- Respectful of their intelligence
- No pressure tactics
